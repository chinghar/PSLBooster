What this tool does — and doesn't do. This measures geometric facial proportions only. These numbers, including the compiled score at the bottom of the Measurements tab, are not a measure of attractiveness, health, or personal worth, and there is no single "ideal" face — the reference ranges shown are figures commonly cited in proportion/aesthetic literature, not a scientific consensus or a target to hit. Apps like this can contribute to unhealthy self-focus if checked often or obsessively; if you notice that happening, consider taking a break.
